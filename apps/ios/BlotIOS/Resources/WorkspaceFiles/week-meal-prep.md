# Week meal prep

For one person · five days · about 90 minutes on Sunday.

## Menu

- Breakfast: overnight oats, yogurt and berries.
- Lunch: chicken rice bowls with peanut, chili-lime or sesame dressing.
- Monday and Thursday: Japanese chicken curry and rice.
- Tuesday and Friday: fresh garlic salmon with vegetables.
- Wednesday: chicken noodle stir-fry.

## Sunday preparation

1. Start four cups of rice in the rice cooker.
2. Roast the chicken thighs at 200°C until cooked through.
3. Roast broccoli and carrots on a second tray.
4. Make the curry on the stove.
5. Prepare oat jars and cucumber slaw while the curry simmers.
6. Divide into containers. Refrigerate the next few days’ portions and freeze the rest.

Cook the fish fresh on Tuesday and Friday.

## Shopping list

- Chicken: 800 g
- Dry rice: 400 g
- Salmon: two portions
- Broccoli, carrots and cucumbers
- Oats, yogurt and berries
- Curry roux, noodles and dressing ingredients
