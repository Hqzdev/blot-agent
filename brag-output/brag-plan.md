# Blot — One task. A whole team.

## Planning rubric
1. Blot is an AI workspace with a coordinator, specialist agents, conversations and files.
2. Strongest product claim: give a task to a team and keep the result in the workspace.
3. Hook: oversized “One task.” is interrupted by the product's animated, colourful agent characters.
4. Actual product material: existing Blot vector characters, animated mark and the Mac team chat/composer/file-card layout, reconstructed in English.
5. Duration: exactly 30 seconds, explicitly requested by the user; overrides brag's 15–25-second default.
6. Tone: polished startup product film, lively and tactile, with spatial continuity instead of presentation slides.
7. Audio: warm rhythmic electronic music, restrained clicks, movement accents and one final soft impact. No narration.
8. Share caption: One message. A whole team on it. Meet Blot — AI teammates that turn a task into work you can open.
9. User flow: send a request → coordinator assigns specialists → agents share sources and findings → receive a launch-plan document.

## Creative contract
English throughout. Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 30.000 seconds. Follow the user's pasted Tuple design reference: #fafafa canvas, #27272a ink, #71717a secondary, #e4e4e7 borders, #18181b dark band, #6a5ed9 only for actions, #3f71d4/#db5434/#1bb152 specialist accents. Inter and technical monospace typography. No narration requested.

This is a designed product demonstration, not a claim that a live competitor research run was executed. All task copy is illustrative; no personal or customer information is used.

## Motion direction
One persistent message is the narrative anchor. Use masked kinetic typography, a composer-to-message shared element, a camera pullback revealing the team, a lateral travelling closeup across three work surfaces, an in-place theme change with routed file packets, a document expansion and a character-to-logo close. Entrances settle quickly; readable text remains while secondary objects continue moving. No carousel of titles and cards, no generic stock footage, no decorative graphs without meaning.
